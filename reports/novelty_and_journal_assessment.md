# Novelty and journal-readiness assessment

Date: 3 October 2026. Manuscript: `lhco2020_pra.tex`. Numerical baseline: release v1.0.1, commit `4b15306cfac9e3f57ace098932db0e4ea69e0f2a`.

## TL;DR

The work has a defensible **incremental empirical and reproducibility contribution**, but no demonstrated new algorithm or theoretical result.
**It is not ready for Physical Review A:** the subject falls outside that journal's scope, and the current evidence does not support a strong novelty claim.
A transparent technical report/preprint is a reasonable present form after independent author review; a research-journal submission needs a focused new finding and stronger validation.

## Method

I compared the implementation, released results, frozen-score bootstrap, archived extensions, and release verification against primary literature and the journals' official scope statements. Searches covered LHC Olympics benchmarking, Deep SVDD/Deep SAD, few-label and weak supervision, conditional density methods, particle representations, and statistical/conformal calibration. The shortlist below includes papers published or posted through 3 October 2026. Recent preprints are labelled separately from peer-reviewed publications.

This is a targeted novelty assessment, not an exhaustive systematic review or proof that no similar study exists. Search-engine coverage, indexing delay, and terminology differences can hide prior work. Novelty judgments below are reasoned assessments of the available evidence; acceptance remains an editorial decision. No new training campaign was performed for this writing task.

## Results

### What this study contributes

**Established within the released experiment:** 47 fresh core fits, common fixed event indices, six five-seed headline settings, a contamination/label grid at seed 42, paired finite-test AUC intervals, topology-transfer measurements, and explicit mass diagnostics. Deep SAD with 100 known anomalies and 0.5% unlabelled contamination reaches mean nominal AUC 0.8880 versus 0.8068 for Isolation Forest; its three-prong mean is 0.7492. The seed-42 paired AUC gain is 0.0596 with conditional 95% interval [0.0570, 0.0625]. These are measured findings, with numerical provenance in `pra_data_audit.json`, `tables/results.csv`, and `tables/auc_bootstrap.json`.

**Plausible incremental contribution:** documenting the joint supervision/contamination/transfer/mass trade-offs for these particular implementations, publishing negative results with a leakage audit, and separating training dispersion from finite-test uncertainty. The exact combination may be useful as a reproducibility resource. It does not, by itself, establish a new scientific principle or satisfy a journal's significance threshold. The community benchmark and detector objectives have substantial prior art, as the shortlist shows.

**Speculative and unsupported as current claims:** universal hypersphere collapse; superiority over actual ANODE or CATHODE; general absence of useful particle-level information; discovery-level false-positive control; a new conformal guarantee; a validated new mass-decorrelated algorithm; quantum advantage. No matching five-seed validation supports the repository's candidate MD-SWAD variants, so the paper excludes them as evaluated contributions. The benchmark contains no quantum method or quantum-information experiment.

### Journal fit and readiness

**Physical Review A: unsuitable in its present subject and not submission-ready.** PRA publishes atomic, molecular, and optical physics, foundations of quantum mechanics, and quantum science, and requires a significant contribution in those areas. This is a classical machine-learning study of simulated hadron-collider events. Using REVTeX's `pra` option supplies the requested format; it does not create subject-matter fit. High desk-rejection risk is my editorial inference from the scope mismatch, not a stated decision by an editor. [Official PRA scope and acceptance criteria](https://journals.aps.org/pra/about).

**Physical Review D: better topical fit, but not currently a strong submission.** Particle-physics methods fall within PRD's scope, but its criteria also require a significant and substantive addition to knowledge. The present work largely benchmarks established methods and leaves major scientific-validation questions unresolved. My assessment is to strengthen the research before approaching PRD; changing the document-class option would not resolve novelty or inference problems. [Official PRD scope and acceptance criteria](https://journals.aps.org/prd/about).

**Journal-worthy in principle, not demonstrated at present.** A rigorous negative-result or reproducibility study can make a substantive contribution without inventing an algorithm, if it establishes a useful, generalizable explanation or corrects a consequential misconception. This project currently offers sample- and implementation-specific observations without the controlled ablations needed for that stronger claim. An academic report or reviewed preprint is a more defensible immediate description than “publication-ready research.”

## Caveats

- The fully supervised model uses all 59,983 nominal training signal labels, despite a `k1000` filename field. Its scaler uses all training events rather than training background only. It is a richer reference, not a 1,000-label fair-budget baseline.
- SVDD's nominal AUC near 0.5 is measured; collapse is not diagnosed. A no-pretraining implementation, center initialization, activation choice, label-batch frequency, and early-stopping objective need controlled investigation before a mechanism claim.
- Most contamination-grid settings have only one seed. Training seeds share the same test events. Bootstrap intervals do not account for model selection, generator/detector systematics, or optimization randomness.
- A deterministic subjettiness cut has nominal AUC about 0.891, stronger than Isolation Forest. Statements that Isolation Forest is best must specify “learned unsupervised core.”
- Fractional acceptance weights are not exact Poisson counts. The sideband procedure uses approximate background uncertainty, bounded fits, limited null trials, and fit-survival conditioning. Large diagnostic Z values must not be presented as discovery significance. The large-sample likelihood approximation has specific assumptions; see Cowan, Cranmer, Gross, Vitells, *Asymptotic formulae for likelihood-based tests of new physics*, 2011, **EPJC 71, 1554**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-011-1554-0).
- Archived particle and density extensions were not rerun with the final core; their sample sizes, preprocessing, and supervision differ. A conformal rank guarantees conservative rejection only under its exchangeability assumptions, not under arbitrary sideband-to-signal-region shift. See Shafer and Vovk, *A Tutorial on Conformal Prediction*, 2008, **JMLR 9, 371–421**, [primary paper](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume9/shafer08a/shafer08a.pdf).
- Historical/fresh neural AUC differences reach approximately 0.211 for the autoencoder and 0.171 for Deep SAD. The fresh environment is canonical, but cross-environment causes and cross-machine portability remain unresolved.
- The earlier closeout gaps about commits, release tags, fast reproduction, and test coverage have been addressed in the release record. That software completion is distinct from independent scientific replication and journal readiness: 30 passing tests and an author-controlled fresh checkout do not certify a new physical result.
- The draft retains the existing author's name and affiliation, includes an AI-use disclosure, and still requires author confirmation of funding/contributions and independent scientific review. APS holds authors responsible for substantive AI-assisted work and specifies disclosure requirements. [APS policy](https://journals.aps.org/authors/appropriate-use-ai-tools).

## Next steps

1. **Choose one testable new question.** The most promising is why the specified SVDD implementation loses nominal ranking and how a small anomalous-label budget changes it. Predefine outcomes and a held-out evaluation protocol before new experiments.
2. **Run mechanism ablations.** Vary pretraining, center initialization, normalization, activations, label exposure, and validation objective with matched data and capacity. Record latent and score dispersion and optimization diagnostics. Repeat the whole relevant grid, rather than only attractive headline settings.
3. **Benchmark faithful strong references.** Include the physics cut and actual conditional-flow density methods; add a particle method with matched supervision. Avoid equating proxy names with published algorithms. Match the supervised scaler in an additional comparison.
4. **Test generalization prospectively.** Use additional masses/topologies and generator or detector variations. Keep a new final evaluation pool untouched during development. Report seed and data-sampling variation separately.
5. **Repair discovery inference before claiming reach.** Use integer randomized acceptance or a justified weighted-data likelihood; define failed-fit outcomes; include threshold and background-model uncertainty; calibrate the full pipeline on enough independent null trials. Account for the look-elsewhere effect if a scan is introduced.
6. **Resolve reproducibility and author review.** Diagnose historical neural differences, obtain an independent cross-machine rerun, and have the author verify all scientific claims and references. Then reassess novelty against updated literature and select a journal whose scope matches the resulting contribution.

The most credible submission story would be an experimentally supported mechanism or a demonstrably valid statistical procedure with broader collider relevance. Cosmetic reformatting or adding more unvalidated models would not supply that evidence.
